One Shooter - free one-shot sample player
by Dani Koenig

This installs the One Shooter VST3 plugin to:
/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3

Requirements: Apple Silicon Mac (M1 or newer), macOS 11 or newer,
a DAW with VST3 support (Bitwig, Ableton Live, Cubase, Studio One, Reaper ...).
